# AI WARP ENGINE 6.4.27 — Hard locks, true LENS screenshots, stronger Neon/ASCII

## Highlights

- **True LENS Freeze Frame:** exports the full LENS canvas (no HUD/frame) as PNG; canvas stays full-size when zooming out. Cross-origin YouTube iframe pixels cannot be read by browsers; use local media, YT LENS or generative sources for those pixels.
- **Hard locks:** voice locks plus new Speed, Opacity, Ghost and V-Sync locks hold regardless of Automated Masters, Continual Randomization, Timed Auto and presets; manual edits update the held value.
- **Neon Gradient:** much stronger rotating ribbon, bloom and colour wash (core and GC#(3)).
- **ASCII GC#(3):** continual full-sheet ASCII stream from the current canvas luminance and glyph sets.
- **Twirl:** smoother, slower, eased warp.
- Stability: guarded resize and effect code paths.
